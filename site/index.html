How it works

The model gets a taskA build box, what must happen, and how it is tested.
It writes a block listEvery block, with its position and exact state.
Real Minecraft runs itA vanilla server places the blocks and flips the levers.
It tries againEach model gets 40 tries and an hour to shrink its build.
Volume is the box around everything a build ever occupies. Blocks drawn with Minecraft's own models and textures.
